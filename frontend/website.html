10 / A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Big decisions.
Welcome questions.

Choosing a school is personal.
We're here to help you feel at home.

Ask us directly ↗
How do I apply for admission?+

Start with our online admission form. For help with availability, documents or the next steps, contact our school office.

Can I visit the school first?+

Yes. Call or WhatsApp 0301-5060341 to discuss a suitable time with our team before your visit.

Where is Beacon Light located?+

Find us in Mohallah Bano Hashim, Tehsil Jand, District Attock. Contact our office if you need directions.

How can families access the school portal?+

Use the portal login with your school-issued account. Contact the office if you need help with access.

Where can I ask about fees and class availability?+

Our school office can share current fee details and class availability. Call 0301-5060341 or email beaconlightjand@gmail.com.
